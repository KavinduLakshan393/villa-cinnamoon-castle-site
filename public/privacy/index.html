Privacy notice

Your inquiry data.

What this reference implementation collects, why it is collected and how it is handled.

Information collected

The inquiry form may collect your name, email address, phone or messaging number, preferred dates, guest count, preferred contact method and message.

Purpose

The information is used only to answer questions about Villa Cinnamoon Castle and to discuss availability, pricing or a potential stay.

Storage and delivery

In this packaged implementation, valid submissions are stored in a private server-side NDJSON file. A production operator may configure an approved webhook to forward the record to the host or a booking workflow.

Analytics

No analytics service is enabled in the packaged project. A future analytics setup must not receive full inquiry messages, email addresses, phone numbers or exact dates tied to a known person.

Retention and rights

The final operator must publish an approved retention period and contact route for deletion or correction requests before production launch.

Security

The server validates submissions, limits repeated requests and uses a hidden spam field. No online payment information is requested.

This notice is an implementation-ready draft and requires owner or legal review before public production use.
