## **🏡 About the Property**

Welcome to **Villa Cinnamoon Castle**\! Tucked away in a serene, green setting just 3.5 km from the lively center of Hikkaduwa town, this spacious two-story villa provides the ideal quiet retreat for large families, group holidays, or remote workers looking to escape the beach crowds while remaining within easy reach of the shore.

The property features generous indoor living areas, a full kitchen, a dedicated workspace, and lush outdoor surroundings with mature trees and private gated parking.
