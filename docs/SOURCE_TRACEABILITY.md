# Source Traceability

The implementation is grounded in the supplied project materials.

## Confirmed published facts

- Brand: Villa Cinnamoon Castle.
- Entire home in Hikkaduwa, Southern Province, Sri Lanka.
- Approximately 3.5 km from Hikkaduwa town and main beach areas.
- Capacity: up to 10 guests.
- Five bedrooms, five beds, and two full bathrooms.
- Bed inventory: four Super King beds and one Queen bed.
- One air-conditioned bedroom; fans in the remaining bedrooms.
- Hot water, full kitchen, dining area, TV, high-speed Wi-Fi, dedicated workspace, washing machine, balcony, porch, garden, and free gated parking.
- Exterior security cameras present.
- New listing with no reviews.
- Smoke and carbon-monoxide alarm status not confirmed.
- No clothes dryer and no central heating.

## Visual implementation decisions

- All factual property imagery comes from the 21-photo selected archive.
- Portrait images are presented in editorial frames instead of being stretched across wide screens.
- Parallax is restrained and progressive; no real-time 3D or synthetic room footage is used.
- Reduced-motion mode removes parallax and large spatial transitions.
- The public location graphic is approximate; no exact coordinate or unverified travel time is published.

## Unresolved owner inputs intentionally omitted

- Public phone, email, and messaging channel.
- Current pricing and availability source.
- Minimum stay, cancellation, children, pet, smoking, party, visitor, and quiet-hour policies.
- Check-in and check-out times.
- Exact map pin and arrival instructions.
- Verified alarm status and camera-location wording.
- Accessibility details and transport services.
- Photo-rights confirmation.
